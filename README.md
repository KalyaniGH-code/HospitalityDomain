# HOSPITALITY DOMAIN 
## Objective:
The project aims to help hotel management teams make informed decisions by visualizing trends and patterns in hotel revenue, occupancy rates, and customer behavior.

## Key Features:
1. Revenue & Occupancy Analysis – Monitor overall revenue performance, occupancy rates, and guest behavior trends over time.
2. Booking and Cancellation Insights – Gain visibility into booking patterns and cancellation metrics by hotel and distribution channel.
3. City-wise Revenue Distribution – Assess revenue contributions across locations to identify high-performing cities.
4. Channel Performance Evaluation – Compare direct bookings with third-party platforms (OTAs) to refine distribution strategies.
5. Interactive Filtering Capabilities – Seamlessly explore data across various time frames, properties, and dimensions.

## Insights from my dashboard:
1. Minor weekday-weekend ADR differences highlight the need for better pricing strategies.
2. Direct offline bookings excel in realization percentage and ADR.
3. Emphasize booking platform enhancements.
4. Low-rated hotels should address their issues.
5. Strong correlations between occupancy, ratings, and cancellations demand rating monitoring.
6. Luxury hotels outperform business hotels in revenue.
7. Elite rooms drive the highest revenue.
8. A Mumbai hotel tops in revenue and ratings.
Overall,  An effective pricing strategy is crucial for revenue growth.
